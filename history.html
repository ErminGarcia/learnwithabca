Our History

More than three decades of faithful, Christ-centered education.

The Beginning

Founding

How a pastor's vision for Biblical education became a school.

A Vision Born

From a Pastor's Heart

ABCA began from the vision of Pastor Rev. Maximo Y. Manay of ABBC, who longed for children to receive consistent Biblical education rooted in Christian truth.

November 1988

A Partnership Formed

Pastor Manay partnered with Accelerated Christian Education Philippines, now known as School of Tomorrow, to establish the school and bring individualized Christian education to the community.

June 1989

Baptist Christian Academy Opens

The school originally operated as Baptist Christian Academy and officially opened its doors in June 1989.

The Early Years

Learning in Church Facilities

During its early years, the school made practical use of available church facilities. The church sanctuary served as a classroom during weekdays, while nearby roads and courts were used for physical activities and sports.

Through the Years

Growth & Achievements

A new name and a growing record of student accomplishments.

July 2000

Becoming Almanza Baptist Christian Academy

In July 2000, the school adopted the name Almanza Baptist Christian Academy (ABCA), reflecting its identity and mission within the Almanza community.

Since 1994

Student Convention Participation

ABCA has participated in School of Tomorrow student conventions since 1994, giving students opportunities to grow through academic, athletic, and skill-based events.

Highlights

Student Accomplishments

Over the years, ABCA students have earned recognition in International Student Convention piano events, achieved success with the girls' volleyball team, and produced strong academic results, including excellent performance in the National Achievement Test and first-place English rankings from 2009 to 2014.

Moving Forward

Expansion & Recognition

Partnerships and milestones that shaped ABCA's development.

Department of Education Recognition

Preschool, Elementary & Secondary Programs

ABCA received Department of Education recognition for its preschool, elementary, and secondary programs, affirming the school's commitment to quality education.

2007

Authorization to Accept Foreign Students

In 2007, ABCA was authorized to accept foreign students, opening its doors to a wider community of learners.

2012

Collaboration with Zion Praise Music

In 2012, ABCA began a collaboration with Zion Praise Music, enriching its music and arts offerings for students.

2018

Practical Spoken English Program

In 2018, the school launched a Practical Spoken English Program to help students build confidence and communication skills.

2019

Model School Status — Supervised

In 2019, ABCA received Model School Status-Supervised recognition from School of Tomorrow Philippines, a meaningful affirmation of its standards and ministry.
