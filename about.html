About ABCA

A Christian, church-based school committed to Biblical education and individualized learning.

Who We Are

A Church-Based Christian School

Almanza Baptist Christian Academy is a Christian, church-related school dedicated to providing Biblical Christian Education. We believe that true education nurtures both the mind and the spirit, and that every child is uniquely gifted by God.

Our school uses the School of Tomorrow / Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) PACE system, an individualized approach that lets students progress through their studies at their own pace. Rather than moving forward as a single class, each learner works through materials suited to their level, with teachers providing guidance, supervision, and support.

Through this approach, ABCA aims to develop academic ability together with character, discipline, responsibility, and Christian leadership. We help students grow in their faith while building the skills they need to serve their families, churches, and communities.

Our Learning System

What Is the PACE System?

The PACE system is the heart of individualized Christian education at ABCA.

The PACE system, associated with Accelerated Christian Education / School of Tomorrow, uses individualized learning materials called PACEs (Packets of Accelerated Christian Education). Each PACE is a self-contained unit of work that guides students through a subject one step at a time.

Students work through their lessons at their own pace, with teachers providing guidance, supervision, and support. This means a child can take the time they need to truly understand a concept before moving on, while faster learners are free to advance without waiting for the rest of a class.

Benefits of the PACE Approach

▲

Self-Paced Progress

Students learn at the speed that suits them, building genuine mastery rather than rushing to keep up.

⚜

Discipline & Responsibility

Working through PACEs helps students develop goal-setting, self-discipline, and accountability.

✿

Biblical Worldview

Each PACE weaves Christian values and Scripture into the subject matter, shaping character alongside knowledge.
