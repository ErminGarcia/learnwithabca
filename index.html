Welcome to ABCA

A Christian School with a Heart for Students

Almanza Baptist Christian Academy is a Christian, church-related school dedicated to Biblical Christian Education and individualized learning.

Christian Foundation

ABCA is a church-based school where Biblical truth is woven into daily learning, helping students build a lasting relationship with Christ.

Individualized Learning

Through the School of Tomorrow ACE PACE system, students work through self-paced materials tailored to their own level and ability.

Character & Discipline

Our approach develops responsibility, discipline, and Christian character alongside strong academic skills.

Our Core Values

At ABCA, we believe that true education goes beyond academics. Our core values guide every aspect of our school community, shaping students into well-rounded individuals who honor God in all they do.

Faith

Christ-Centered Living

We prioritize a personal relationship with Jesus Christ, encouraging students to integrate biblical principles into their daily lives and decision-making.

Academics

Excellence in Learning

We strive for academic excellence through the rigorous and individualized ACE PACE curriculum, empowering students to reach their full intellectual potential.

Character

Integrity and Service

We cultivate strong moral character, teaching students to act with honesty, respect, and a servant's heart toward their peers and the wider community.
