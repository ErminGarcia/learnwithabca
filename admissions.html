Programs We Offer

Our Program Offerings

Individualized, Christ-centered education from early childhood through junior high school.

Grades 7–10

Junior High School

Our Junior High School program for Grades 7–10 uses individualized, self-paced learning to prepare students for higher education and future careers while maintaining a strong Christian foundation. Students grow in independence, responsibility, and faith as they work through their academic journey.

Grades 1–6

Intermediate

Our Grades 1–6 program focuses on individualized, self-paced learning while building academic skills, character, responsibility, discipline, and preparation for higher levels of education. Children develop at their own pace within a nurturing, Christ-centered environment.

Preschool & Kindergarten

Preschool & Kindergarten

ABCA provides Preschool and Kindergarten education designed to prepare young children for formal schooling while introducing a strong Christian foundation through age-appropriate and engaging learning. It is a warm first step into a lifetime of Christ-centered education.
