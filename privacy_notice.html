Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Almanza Baptist Christian Academy collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information submitted through this website.

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. About This Notice

Almanza Baptist Christian Academy ("ABCA," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Notice applies to personal information collected through the ABCA website, including information submitted through our contact, inquiry, and expression-of-interest forms.

2. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit information through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Student's full name
  • Student's age
  • Student's grade level
  • Email address
  • Contact number
  • Additional information that you choose to provide

We may also receive basic technical information necessary for the operation, security, and reliability of the website.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive or confidential information through website forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website may be used for legitimate school-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries from parents, guardians, students, or visitors.
  • Responding to admissions and enrollment-related questions.
  • Following up on expressions of interest submitted through the website.
  • Providing information about the school's admissions process.
  • Communicating with prospective families when necessary.
  • Managing and maintaining website inquiry records.
  • Protecting the security and proper operation of the website.

We do not intentionally collect more information than is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

4. Information About Students

Some website forms may request information relating to students, including a student's name, age, and grade level.

We recognize that students may be minors and therefore take additional care when handling information relating to children.

Parents or legal guardians should provide information about children where appropriate and should avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for the purpose of the inquiry.

5. Who May Access Your Information

Personal information submitted through the website may be accessed by authorized ABCA personnel who require the information to respond to inquiries, assist with admissions-related matters, or perform legitimate administrative responsibilities.

We may also use trusted technology and service providers to operate parts of our website and information systems. These providers may process information on our behalf when necessary to provide their services.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website uses certain third-party technologies and services to provide its functionality.

Supabase

We use Supabase for database and authentication-related functionality. Information submitted through certain website forms may be stored using Supabase.

Google Maps

Our Contact page may display Google Maps to help visitors locate the school. Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies when its services are used.

Content Delivery Services

Some website resources may be loaded from third-party content delivery services. These services may receive technical information associated with requests made by your browser.

7. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no website, online service, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, for legitimate school or administrative purposes, or when retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or securely disposed of in accordance with our applicable procedures.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Philippine law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is processed.
  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain forms of processing where applicable.
  • Request deletion or removal of information where applicable.
  • Raise concerns regarding the handling of your personal information.

Requests concerning information relating to a minor may be made by the student's parent or legal guardian where permitted by law.

10. Website Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by third parties.

We recommend reviewing the privacy notices of external services before providing them with personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our website, practices, services, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated notice will be published on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.
